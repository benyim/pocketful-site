Meet Pocket

Solve puzzles.
Collect cats.

Pocketful is built for calm, unhurried puzzling. Pick a game, take as long as you like, and let the board be the only thing you are thinking about.

Pocket is the cat who comes along for it. Keep solving and you collect his stickers as you go: a new one every week, more as your solves add up, and over a hundred to find.

Daily Challenges

The daily bends the rules.

Every game's daily challenge plays a twist on the game you know. Sudoku grows thermometers. Nonograms fog over. Stars come in pairs, and bridges cross exactly once.

A fresh board in every game, every morning. Solve any one of them and your streak lives another day.
