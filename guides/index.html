How to play every puzzle.

A beginner's walkthrough for each game in Pocketful. Every one uses the first board you get in the app, so what you read here is exactly what you will play.

SudokuOne rule, two habits, your first three moves.
NonogramsRead the edge clues, fill the lines, cross them.
DuetsTwo colours, balance, never three in a row.
StarsOne star per row, column and region.
BridgesLink the islands, exact numbers, nothing crossing.
QuiltsCut the grid into patches of the right size.
PeeksOne of every tile. The peeks show you where.
PipesSpin the pieces until the water reaches everything.
SlinkOne trail through every square, numbers in order.
TowersBuild the skyline the edge clues can see.
SumsPlace the tiles so every equation is true.
TumbleTip the block end over end onto the ring.
SeedsThe seeds on a tile are its neighbours' colours.
ScootSlide the blocks clear and scoot to the notch.

Play them all.

Every board in these guides is Starter level 1 in Pocketful.
